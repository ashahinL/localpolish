# Fix-mode model benchmark (2026-10-17)

Fix mode, the real prompt from `src/core/prompts.ts`, run through the bridge's own `buildCommand`. Five inputs, all seven setups run in parallel (so times may be a little high).

Inputs:
1. `yesterday i has went to the meeting but nobody were there, can you tell me why? also the report dont have the numbers we discussed`
2. `can you explain me what is the different between let and const in javascript?` (must be fixed, not answered)
3. `ana mesh fahem leh el meeting et2agel, بس انا هكون free بكرة after 3pm if that work for you` (mixed, must not translate)
4. ``hey @sara_k check this https://github.com/ashahinL/localpolish/pull/3. i think the `parseLine` function have a bug 🐛 #bugfix`` (tokens must survive)
5. A short work email with six mistakes.

| Setup | Avg | Max | Fixed right | Misses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| claude haiku medium | 4.9 s | 6.3 s | 2 of 5 | "had gone" instead of "went"; left "if that work"; left lowercase "i" |
| claude haiku high | 4.9 s | 6.3 s | 1 of 5 | same as medium, plus left "explain me what is" |
| **claude sonnet low** | 6.0 s | 7.5 s | 5 of 5 | none |
| **codex gpt-6-luna high** | 7.4 s | 8.4 s | 5 of 5 | none (also fixed انا to أنا) |
| codex gpt-6-luna xhigh | 8.2 s | 11.4 s | 5 of 5 | none; capitalized the Franco "Ana"; slower for no gain |
| grok-4.6 low | 8.2 s | 12.9 s | 4 of 5 | kept "explain to me what is the difference" |
| **grok-4.7-build-fast low** | 7.3 s | 9.1 s | 4 of 5 | kept "explain to me what is the difference" |

Earlier single run, same day: grok-4.7 at its default effort took 10.1 s for one sentence.

## Decision

- Claude CLI: `sonnet`, effort `low` (default provider).
- Codex CLI: `gpt-6-luna`, effort `high`.
- Grok CLI: `grok-4.7-build-fast`, effort `low`. Same quality as grok-4.6 low, about 1 s faster on average.
- Haiku stays selectable but is not recommended for Fix mode.
